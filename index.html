      Move your data to another device

      Your data is saved only in this browser. To use it somewhere else, export a backup here, then import it on the other device from this same page.

      1
      Export from this device

      Download saves a .json file. Copy puts the same backup on your clipboard so you can paste it into a note, email or message to yourself.

      2
      Import on the other device

      You can merge it with what is already there, or replace everything.

      Spreadsheet export

      Every food and exercise entry as a CSV file you can open in Excel or Google Sheets. This is for viewing, not for importing back.
